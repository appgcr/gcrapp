## App Store

**Name (30):** GCR Valuation

**Subtitle (30):** Bank property valuation

**Promotional text (170):**
Bank requests, site visits, AI-checked documents and digitally signed reports in each bank's own format — one app for banks, branch offices and field engineers.

**Keywords (100):**
valuation,property,bank,valuer,site visit,engineer,report,loan,mortgage,survey,inspection,Kadapa

**Description:**
GCR Valuation is the working app of GCR, an approved panel valuer in Andhra Pradesh. It connects bank officers, GCR branch offices and field engineers so every property valuation moves from request to signed report in one place.

FOR BANK OFFICERS
• Send a valuation request with the customer, property and loan details
• Follow every step live: received, engineer assigned, site visit, review, report ready
• Download the approved report in your bank's own format

FOR FIELD ENGINEERS
• See the site visits assigned to you
• Upload the sale deed, building plan, tax receipt and market value certificate — the documents are checked automatically
• Take geo-tagged, time-stamped site photos
• Enter measurements and sign the report once with your saved digital signature

FOR BRANCH ADMINS
• Receive bank requests and assign engineers
• Review, return or approve reports
• Manage your team, letterhead and valuer details

BUILT FOR TRUST
• Role-based access: everyone sees only their own work
• Real GPS on site photos
• Reports fingerprinted so changes can be detected
• Location is shared only while an engineer is on duty

Accounts are created by GCR for its staff and partner bank officers. If you need access, contact GCR at hello.happydooo@gmail.com.
